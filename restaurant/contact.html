Guest Guidance

Frequently Answered Queries

Essential information regarding reservations, dietary adaptations, and cellar protocols.

Table bookings open exactly 60 calendar days in advance at 09:00 GMT on the first day of each month. Due to our intimate 34-seat dining room, dinner seatings between 19:00 and 20:30 frequently book rapidly. Priority waitlists are maintained by our concierge.
With 48 hours advance notice, Chef Julian Mercier and our brigade prepare parallel bespoke iterations of the tasting journey for vegetarian, pescatarian, gluten-free, and dairy-free guests without compromising culinary rigor. We kindly ask that life-threatening allergies be noted upon initial reservation.
Our attire guideline is elegant smart casual. Jackets are encouraged for gentlemen, though not mandatory. We respectfully request that guests refrain from sportswear, athletic sneakers, and beach footwear.
We permit up to one 750ml bottle of commemorative vintage wine per two guests, provided the specific vintage is not currently featured on our reserve cellar list. A corkage and stemware service fee of ₹1,200 per bottle applies.
Reservations may be rescheduled or cancelled without penalty up to 48 hours prior to service. Cancellations occurring within 48 hours or unnotified non-arrivals incur a fee of ₹1,500 per guest to honor the morning procurement of perishable wild provisions.
