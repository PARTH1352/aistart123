Our Heritage · Founded 2018

The Art of Silent Luxury

Born from a dialogue between classic culinary rigor and Japanese wabi-sabi aesthetics, Selfwise Cafe was conceived as a respite from the noise of modern dining.

Chapter I · The Genesis

From Kyoto to Mayfair

After twelve years heading three-star Michelin establishments across Paris and Lyon, Chef Patron Julian Mercier spent two winters in Kyoto studying kaiseki dining under Master Tadamichi. He became captivated by the Japanese reverence for shun — the exact micro-moment when an ingredient reaches peak vitality.

Returning in 2018, Mercier envisioned Selfwise Cafe: a 34-seat sanctum stripped of dining hall ostentation, gold leaf, and loud theater. Here, the room’s dark stone walls and acoustic wool panels absorb sound so that only the sizzle of embers, coffee blooms, and quiet conversations remain.

"When you silence the distractions, the subtlety of a smoked broth or forty-day aged beef vibrates with unprecedented clarity."

Chapter II · The Materials

The Artisan Guild

A dish begins long before it enters the kitchen. Our ceramic vessels are individually thrown by master potter Kenjiro Sato in Shigaraki, using high-iron clay fired with red pine wood over seven days.

Our cutlery is forged by seventh-generation blade-smiths in Thiers, France, balanced with unpolished dark bog oak handles harvested from sunken peat bogs. Every tactile touchpoint echoes our devotion to natural textures.

In the cellar, Sommelier Elena Vance guards 3,200 bottles focused purely on biodynamic, small-grower estates where grapevines are tended by horse rather than tractor.

A Chronology of Precision

Milestones in Gastronomy

A relentless eight-year journey of refinement, accolades, and environmental responsibility.

2018

The Foundation at Albemarle Street

Selfwise Cafe opens its doors after two years of acoustic and architectural craftsmanship, welcoming just twenty-four guests nightly.

2020

First Michelin Star Distinction

Awarded our inaugural Michelin Star within fourteen months of operation, alongside the National Restaurant Awards' "Best Newcomer in the UK".

2022

Two Michelin Stars & The Green Clover

Elevated to Two Michelin Stars and bestowed the Michelin Green Star for pioneering zero-waste fermentation and direct farm stewardship.

2024

The Pinnacle: Three Michelin Stars

Recognized with the highest honor in gastronomy: Three Michelin Stars. The Michelin Guide praised our "unmatched purity and technical emotional depth".

2026

World's 50 Best & Forbes 5-Star

Voted #14 in The World's 50 Best Restaurants and receiving unanimous 5-Star Forbes ratings, continuing to evolve our culinary vocabulary.

Julian Mercier

Trained at L'Arpège in Paris and Kikunoi in Kyoto. Julian leads the kitchen brigade with architectural discipline, developing all seasonal recipes in our subterranean laboratory.

Former Best Sommelier of the UK. Elena curates our subterranean 3,200-bottle vault with an uncompromising commitment to low-intervention growers and forgotten indigenous varietals.
