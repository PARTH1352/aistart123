## 2. Updated Branding & Typography (Bold & Impactful)

- **Name**: **SELFWISE CAFE**
- **Subtitle**: `ARTISANAL GASTRONOMIE & ROASTERY`
- **Typography Weights**:
  - Brand Logo: `font-weight: 800` Cormorant Garamond Serif
  - Headings (`h1`, `h2`, `h3`, `h4`): `font-weight: 700`
  - Dish Titles & Prices: `font-weight: 700` with punchy Indian Rupee (₹) formatting
  - Buttons & Navigation: `font-weight: 700`
  - Strict Minimal Color Scheme preserved (Zero Gradients)
